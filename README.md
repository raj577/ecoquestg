## ✨ Project Overview

[cite_start]EcoRhythm is an interactive, practical digital platform designed to address the theoretical nature of environmental education in India and the lack of engaging tools for eco-friendly practices[cite: 17, 19, 25].

[cite_start]Our goal is to focus on **habit-building** and **long-term behavioral change**, making sustainability engaging and rewarding[cite: 25, 27]. [cite_start]We aim to create a generation with a genuine sense of ownership over their ecological footprint[cite: 23].

## 💡 The Problem

* [cite_start]Environmental education in India is primarily theoretical[cite: 17].
* [cite_start]There is minimal focus on real-world applications and local ecological issues[cite: 18].
* [cite_start]Students often lose interest because the learning feels theoretical, and the long-term impact of their actions (e.g., a neglected sapling or improperly segregated waste) is often lost[cite: 22].
* [cite_start]This results in a lack of engaging tools for practicing eco-friendly habits[cite: 19].

## 🚀 Our Solution: EcoRhythm

EcoRhythm is a platform that makes environmental action:
* [cite_start]**Personal:** Users take responsibility through daily habits[cite: 27].
* [cite_start]**Rewarding:** Through gamification, including points, certificates, and recognition[cite: 27].
* [cite_start]**Sustainable:** By fostering long-term behavioral change and national-scale habit formation[cite: 27, 119].
